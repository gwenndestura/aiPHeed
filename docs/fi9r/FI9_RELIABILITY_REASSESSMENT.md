## Final decision: answers

**1. Is FI9 reliable enough for a provisional operational outlook?**  
Yes, as a provisional regional outlook clearly labelled as such. It is reproducible, uses only data published by the issue date (audits pass), and its probabilities are reasonably calibrated below 0.2 (ECE 0.073). It is no better than repeating the latest reading, and the card says so.

**2. Has it demonstrated predictive skill beyond simple baselines?**  
Beyond climatology/always-LOW: yes on Brier (0.118 vs 0.149) and balanced accuracy (0.714 vs 0.500). Beyond naive persistence: **no** (Brier 0.118 vs 0.115).

**3. Is its Lead-1 forecast reliable across survey conditions?**  
No. Without the sawtooth quarters recall falls to 0.444 and the reliability rule fails. Since 2023, 300-respondent rounds read about 5 points lower than larger ones.

**4. Is the 75 % accuracy objective achieved without misleading class imbalance?**  
No. Accuracy 0.823 equals always-LOW (0.823). The informative measures are balanced accuracy 0.714 (CI 0.50–0.83) and recall 0.545 (5 of 11 HIGH quarters missed).

**5. Is the projected hunger percentage statistically defensible?**  
Only as a persistence-based projection (MAE 4.17 points), shown as such. It is not a model of causes and it is not CALABARZON's rate.

**6. Is the 95 % interval empirically reliable?**  
The old Gaussian interval was not (89% coverage). The conformal interval now served covered 97% of past outcomes. That was measured on the quarters used to adopt it, so it is labelled a 'likely range' and tracked prospectively.

**7. Can the current output be interpreted as a CALABARZON forecast?**  
No. It is Balance Luzon, of which CALABARZON is about a third. No repeated CALABARZON outcome series exists.

**8. Can any provincial or LGU food-insecurity forecast be scientifically supported?**  
No. There is no repeated provincial or municipal outcome. LGU figures are a vulnerability priority.

**9. Did the new provincial dataset contribute to a valid new training or validation process?**  
It contributed verification (40/50 records confirmed exactly against primary sources), the decision that no provincial model is possible, and a historical provincial reference now shown separately on the dashboard. It was not used for training, because no record is quarterly or comparable across years. The 2015 cross-check of the LGU priority is weak (rank correlation 0.30, n = 5).

**10. Should the current model remain in production or be replaced?**  
Remain. retain C0 (FI9 damped persistence): no candidate passed every adoption check. Only the interval method changed, by the locked rule.

**11. What exact evidence is still required before FI9 can be described as reliable?**  
At least 12 prospective scored forecasts with at least 3 HIGH quarters that pass the reliability rule and are not worse than persistence. Also SWS documentation (or microdata) explaining the 300- vs 600-respondent rounds, and recorded release dates for pre-2021 rounds. A CALABARZON claim would further need a repeated CALABARZON outcome (e.g. NNS/CBMS household data).
