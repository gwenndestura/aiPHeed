## Feasibility of a provincial model

* **Quarterly provincial forecasting: not feasible.** No source gives repeated provincial outcomes.
* **Provincial estimation at survey dates (SAE):** feasible in principle for 2023, if the NNS PUF has province identifiers, weights and FIES items. Province samples of ~384 households give direct estimates with roughly ±5–7 point CIs, and POPCEN 2024 / PSA small-area poverty can serve as covariates. That is a cross-sectional validation, not a forecast. It would need its own pipeline (`app/fi_labels/hfias.py` is ready for the household files).
