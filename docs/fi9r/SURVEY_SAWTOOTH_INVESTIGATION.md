## 5. Conclusion

The recent sawtooth is largely a survey-design effect (300- vs 600-respondent rounds), measured at about 5 points with p ≈ 0.04 on 14 rounds. It cannot be separated cleanly from genuine change or respondent composition, and the older sawtooth cannot be attributed at all. No adjustment is applied to the target. The effect is disclosed on the dashboard. Resolving it needs SWS documentation of the two round types (sampling design, timing, sponsorship) or the round microdata.
