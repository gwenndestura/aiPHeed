# Does news improve aiPHeed's forecasts? Reassessment by horizon (FI9-N)

## Correction to an earlier statement

FI6-N's 'Lead 1' was described in this session as a current-quarter nowcast. That was imprecise. FI6-N issues on the **last day of quarter t** and targets **quarter t+1**. By the project's own definition (target = the calendar quarter after the issue quarter), that is a **next-quarter** forecast. It differs from the served FI9 Lead 1 in two ways:
* it is issued one month later (end of quarter vs end of the quarter's 2nd month);
* its news covers the complete issue quarter, instead of the last complete quarter before issue.

Both read the same SWS quarter (the latest reading is about two quarters old in both). This study compares the horizons on identical issue dates.

## 4. Conclusions

**Does news improve next-quarter forecasting?** Yes for probability quality when the forecast is issued on the last day of the quarter (QE1). Adding news to the government model lowers Brier by 0.0099. The interval stays below zero:
* at 95 % and under the 5-horizon Bonferroni correction (-0.0099 [-0.0247, -0.0005]);
* without the sawtooth quarters;
* in both eras with news coverage (most of the gain is in 2021–2026).

AUC rises by +0.062, and calibration error falls from 0.028 to 0.012. The information is spread across domains: removing any one domain costs 0.002–0.005 Brier, with poverty news largest.

At the FI9 issue date (OP1) the gain is not significant (-0.0013 [-0.0051, +0.0023]), and fresher 90-day news windows do not restore it (-0.0001 [-0.0056, +0.0071]). A possible explanation, not tested here, is that the useful news falls in the last month of the issue quarter.

**Does news improve current-quarter nowcasting?** No (-0.0037 [-0.0066, +0.0035]).

**Does news improve probability quality even when accuracy does not improve?** Yes, at QE1:
* Government + news makes exactly the same HIGH/LOW calls as government only, with better probabilities.
* News + latest reading catches one more HIGH quarter (accuracy 0.839 → 0.855). That one-quarter difference is not significant (McNemar p = 1.00).

**Does news add value beyond the latest SWS observation?** No. At every horizon, the best news configuration is not better than naive persistence. At QE1: +0.0027 [-0.0273, +0.0396] for government + news, and +0.0037 [-0.0248, +0.0351] for news + latest reading. Persistence passes the FI7 reliability rule; no news model does.

**Is the improvement statistically and operationally meaningful?** It is statistically meaningful, narrowly, for LightGBM probability quality at QE1. It is not operationally meaningful under the locked rule: no news configuration both passes the reliability rule and beats persistence. The served FI9 forecaster therefore stays damped persistence. News remains a measured, documented contributor to model probability quality at the quarter-end issue, not to the served next-quarter forecast.

**Sawtooth side-finding.** At the current-quarter nowcast (OP0) the latest reading is only one quarter old, and naive persistence is worse (Brier 0.187 vs 0.116 at QE1). Since 2010, a reading two quarters old has been closer to the outcome than a reading one quarter old (mean absolute difference 4.16 vs 4.76 points), which is the alternating survey pattern documented in docs/fi9r/SURVEY_SAWTOOTH_INVESTIGATION.md.
