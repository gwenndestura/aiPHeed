# Search for ENNS 2022 provincial outcomes (2026-11-06; after all five candidates were sealed)

**Sealed before this search:** E158/E166 `49e514d2`, E168 `f8228fae`, E170 `af0180af`, E171 `a929929a`.

## Found
**"2022 Epidemiologic Follow-up Study of the 2019 ENNS"** (DOST-FNRI):
- Source: web archive copy of `enutrition.fnri.dost.gov.ph/uploads/2022 Epidemiologic Follow up Study of the 2019 ENNS.pdf`, 9.6 MB, sha256 `b20c926c…`. Not stored in the repo.
- **This is the 2022 round the seals target.** It covers 20 areas randomly chosen from the 2019 ENNS, with fieldwork from July 2022, and is a panel of 23,410 matched 2019 and 2022 households.
- **The 20 areas:** Lanao del Norte, Lanao del Sur, South Cotabato, Kalinga, Davao del Norte, Marikina City, Quirino, Zamboanga Sibugay, Zamboanga del Sur, Puerto Princesa City, Marinduque, Parañaque City, **Rizal**, Pampanga, Pangasinan, Benguet, Ilocos Norte, Southern Leyte, Cebu, Malabon City.
- **Food security is reported only pooled** across the 20 areas, as HFIAS "food insecure" **including mild** (2019 60.7 %; 2022 about 52 %).
- **No provincial values and no moderate + severe split are printed.**

## Not found

## Consequence
- **No sealed forecast can be scored.** The pooled "any food insecurity" figure is a different measure (it includes mild) and is not provincial.
- **The maximum reliable lead stays 0 for every model.**
- **The decisive outcome data exist but are unpublished online:** 2022 provincial HFIAS moderate + severe for the 20 areas above (**16 provinces** that all five seals cover, including **Rizal**; the other 4 are cities outside the provincial panel). FNRI holds them, and the provincial nutrition offices may have received them at dissemination.
  - Obtaining them would need a request to DOST-FNRI or to the provincial offices. This is the owner's decision (standing rule: public data only, unless the owner decides otherwise).
