## Answers

**1. Did the supplied Philippine labels contribute?**  
Yes, to validation, not training. FIES showed SWS hunger is a different construct (area ρ = −0.8; opposite 2019–21 movement). FNRI 2015 HFIAS validated the priority layer (poverty ρ = 1.0 vs current priority 0.30). An auxiliary cross-sectional task was measured (LOO MAE 0.60 vs 2.79).

**2. Which labels were used / excluded?**  
Used: FNRI 2015 HFIAS, PSA FIES (national and regional), PSA subsistence (as food poverty). Excluded: the 10 ENNS provincial records (unverified, inconsistent scale).

**3. Food insecurity rather than price pressure?**  
Yes: the target is SWS involuntary hunger, a direct measure, regional. It must be described as involuntary hunger, not FIES food insecurity.

**4. Best fusion strategy?**  
At quarter end: early fusion in LightGBM with the simple stress index or the FI6-N features (-0.0101 [-0.0186, -0.0021], P 1.00). At the FI9 issue timing: none.

**5. Did news improve accuracy?**  
Not reliably. At quarter end, LightGBM + stress index accuracy is 0.694 vs 0.661, both below persistence (0.871).

**6. Recall and balanced accuracy?**  
At quarter end: recall 0.64 → 0.73, balanced accuracy 0.652 → 0.707 (LightGBM). Not at the FI9 timing.

**7. Brier and calibration?**  
Yes for LightGBM at quarter end (Brier -0.0078 [-0.0150, -0.0015], P 1.00). No at the FI9 timing.

**8. Beyond government data alone?**  
Yes, at quarter end, for LightGBM probabilities only.

**9. Beyond persistence?**  
No. No news configuration beats naive persistence at either timing.

**10. Survives chronological validation and sensitivity?**  
The quarter-end LightGBM gain is significant across the full walk-forward and (FI9-N) both eras and the sawtooth exclusion. It does not survive an issue one month earlier.

**11. At least 75 % accuracy?**  
Production 0.823; naive persistence 0.871.

**12. Meaningfully better than baselines?**  
No: always-LOW is 0.823, and no model beats naive persistence on Brier.

**13. Overfitting?**  
The production model does not overfit (3 parameters). LightGBM and logistic models with government features do.

**14. Generalizes across periods?**  
Partly. Balanced accuracy before 2021 is 0.559 (production) / 0.673 (naive), and from 2021 0.810 / 0.798. Every model weakens without the sawtooth quarters.

**15. Across provinces?**  
Not testable: no provincial outcome series.

**16. Maximum reliable lead?**  
None (robust rule). Lead 1 passes on full data but not without the sawtooth quarters; Lead 2 has no useful skill (AUC 0.33–0.66; no model beats climatology on Brier).

**17. Is Lead 1 reliable?**  
Provisional. It passes the reliability rule on all and recent quarters, not across survey-design conditions.

**18. Calibrated probabilities?**  
Reasonably (ECE 0.073); mid-range probabilities have been too low.

**19. Intervals defensible?**  
Conformal: 60/62 covered (exact CI 0.89–1.00). Labelled a likely range.

**20. Geographically appropriate for DA IV-A?**  
Only as regional context: Balance Luzon (CALABARZON is ~34 %). FIES shows the SWS pattern differs from food-insecurity rates.

**21. Weaknesses corrected?**  
Timing confusion about news resolved and reproduced; news representation rebuilt (event types, windows, momentum, recency); attention bias identified; design effect separated from seasonality; labels used for target and priority validation; challenger register started.

**22. Weaknesses remaining?**  
Few HIGH quarters; SWS design effect (−6.5 pp, cause unknown); regional, not CALABARZON; SWS ≠ FIES; no provincial series; news archive dominated by one source; historical folds already seen.

**23. Replace production?**  
retain production C0: no configuration passed every adoption check.
