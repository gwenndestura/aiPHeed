* Nationally the two moved in **opposite directions from 2019 to 2021** (FIES down, SWS up during the pandemic), and in the same direction from 2021 to 2023.
* Across the four SWS areas in 2023 the rank correlation is **-0.80** (n = 4). SWS hunger is highest in Metro Manila; FIES is highest in Mindanao.
* CALABARZON FIES 2023 = 19.1 %; the mean of Balance Luzon regions = 26.7 %; SWS Balance Luzon 2023 = 11.15 %.

**Conclusion [S, small n]:**
* SWS involuntary hunger (severe deprivation, one question, past 3 months) and FIES moderate-or-severe insecurity (8 items, 12 months) are different constructs, with different spatial patterns.
* aiPHeed's forecast must be described as **involuntary hunger**, not FIES food insecurity.
* This is the strongest legitimate use of the FIES labels: it constrains what the forecast can claim.
