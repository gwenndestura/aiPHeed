# Objective 5 / H4 / H5: analysis plan, fixed before any response is collected

1. **Valid response:**
   - consent given;
   - DA Region IV-A personnel;
   - at least 80% of the Likert items answered.

   Exclusions are reported with their reasons. **No response is invented or imputed.**
2. **Per characteristic** (Parts B–E):
   - per item: median, mean, SD and the % agree (4–5);
   - per characteristic: the mean of its items.

   Verbal interpretation: 1.00–1.80 Strongly disagree, 1.81–2.60 Disagree, 2.61–3.40 Neutral, 3.41–4.20 Agree,
   4.21–5.00 Strongly agree.
3. **Reliability:** Cronbach's α per characteristic with ≥ 3 items, and overall; α ≥ 0.70 acceptable. Item–total
   correlations; an item with r < 0.30 is reported, not removed after the fact.
4. **H5** (functional suitability, accuracy, usability favorable):
   - **rejected** for a characteristic if its respondent mean is significantly above 3.40, by a one-sample Wilcoxon
     signed-rank test, one-sided, α = 0.05, Holm across the three characteristics;
   - the effect size r is reported.
5. **H4** (LGU rankings consistent with evaluators):
   - provinces: each evaluator's ranking vs aiPHeed's province order, by Spearman ρ; median ρ across evaluators, and a
     one-sided sign test that ρ > 0;
   - agreement among evaluators: Kendall's W;
   - LGUs: the overlap of each evaluator's top-10 with aiPHeed's top-10, against the hypergeometric chance expectation
     (10 × 10 / 142 ≈ 0.70), by a one-sided Wilcoxon test;
   - H4 is **rejected** if both province ρ and the LGU overlap exceed chance at α = 0.05.
6. **ISO/IEC 25010:2023 mapping:** usability → interaction capability; the other names are unchanged; safety is not
   assessed.
7. **Reporting:** all items, n per item, the excluded count, and verbatim open-ended themes (coded by two members, κ
   reported).
