# LGU weighting: evidence for the adviser's decision (D3.2; nothing changed in the served index)

## What each quantity measures

| Quantity | Measures | Does not measure |
|---|---|---|
| Poverty incidence (PSA 2021 SAE, %) | The share of people below the poverty line: **vulnerability** | How many people are affected |
| Number of poor people (incidence × 2024 population) | **Exposure:** how many poor people live there | How deep the poverty is |
| Population density (2024 population / km²) | Crowding, urbanisation | Poverty. It is negatively related to poverty (Spearman −0.53) |
| Vulnerability | The likelihood of being harmed by a food shock | Whether harm occurred (no municipal hunger data) |

## Options

| Option | Formula (within province, min-max; × province score) | Top 5 | Advantages | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Served (Objective 3 text) | 0.6 poverty + 0.4 density | Taytay, Lipa, San Francisco, Macalelon, San Andres | Matches the approved wording | Density lifts dense, less-poor towns (Taytay 9.8%, Lipa 12.0%) above rural Quezon towns at about 25%. Top-10 mean poverty 19.3% |
| B. Poverty only | poverty | San Francisco, Macalelon, San Andres, Infanta, Panukulan | The clearest vulnerability measure; top-10 mean poverty 25.3% | Ignores how many people are affected; τ 0.71 vs served |
| C. Poverty + exposure | 0.6 poverty + 0.4 poor-people count | San Francisco, Infanta, Antipolo, Lopez, Pagbilao | Keeps large poor urban populations (Antipolo) without rewarding density itself; top-10 mean 22.8% | The 0.4 weight is a choice, not estimated |
| D. Two dimensions, not combined | Report the vulnerability rank and the exposure rank side by side | Vulnerability: San Francisco, Macalelon… Exposure: large cities | No arbitrary weight. Shows that they disagree (τ 0.15) | Two lists instead of one |

## Implications for the thesis

- **Objective 3 specifies "poverty and density weights".** Options B, C and D change the method, so the thesis text
  must be updated if one is chosen.
- **The evidence does not support density as a vulnerability factor in CALABARZON**, because it runs against poverty.
- **Whichever option is chosen, ranks are coarse** (90% interval about 51 places) and must stay labelled as estimates.

**Decision needed from the adviser:** A, B, C or D. **Nothing is changed until then.**
