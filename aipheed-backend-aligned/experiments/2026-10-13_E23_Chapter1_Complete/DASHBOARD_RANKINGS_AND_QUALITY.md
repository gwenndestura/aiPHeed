# Dashboard, rankings, face validity and technical quality

## Development dashboard (`dashboard_dev/`, isolated copy; nothing deployed)

**Copy.** A copy of E18's dashboard: E18's `meeting-materials` folder was removed; `node_modules` is a read-only junction to `frontend/node_modules`; the port is 8099.

**New page: `/forecast-period`** (`src/pages/ForecastPeriod.tsx`, helpers in `src/lib/e23.ts`). It shows:

- **Period banner:** "Forecast target: Q1 2027 (January–March 2027)", "Supporting news: Q4 2026 (October–December 2026)", the cutoff and the status. **There is no forecast while the status is INSUFFICIENT_DATA**, and the page lists the missing items.
- **News evidence:** filtered on the client by the same window as the record. Articles after the cutoff or undated are never shown.
- **Per-province counts** with LIMITED_SIGNAL.
- **Evaluation table** by lead and target definition: accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, Brier and the confusion counts for the proposed model, the six baselines and the PSA-only supplement. Also the E23-B result beside always-HIGH.
- **Hypotheses**, the **142 rankings** (back-test example, poverty-source column), the NNS anchor, and the **PDF download**.

**Single source.** The JSON (`public/e23_dashboard.json`) and the PDF (`public/aiPHeed_forecast_report_2027-Q1.pdf`) are both generated by `runner/build_outputs.py` from the same forecast record, the one the API endpoint serves.

## Rankings (Objective 3)

- **142/142 LGUs.** An independent re-implementation gives the same risk index as the backend `Disaggregator` (maximum absolute difference 0.0), with identical order.
- **Missing provinces.** After fix F2, a province without a forecast yields `null` / `NO_PROVINCE_FORECAST` and sorts last.
- **Poverty values.** 126 LGUs use the province-mean poverty value. For them, the 0.6 poverty weight is constant within the province and only density orders them. Every row discloses this.
- **2027-Q1 ranking: unavailable** (no province forecast). The dashboard shows the 2026-Q1 back-test ranking, labelled as such.
- **What the rankings are.** Relative ordinal estimates. They are not official classifications and not independent municipal forecasts.
