**What it holds.**
- PSA provincial labour-market estimates for Batangas, Cavite, Laguna, Quezon and Rizal: annual averages, 2021-2025.
- 2023-2025 are marked preliminary in source. 2026 is not yet available.

**Checks run here.**
- Employment rate + unemployment rate = 100 where both are given (Laguna 2023: 100.1, rounding).
- The unemployment and underemployment rates match the person counts to 0.1 pp for 2021-2023.

**Gaps.**
- No 2024 unemployment rate. It equals 100 − employment rate, which is derivable but must be labelled derived.
- Batangas 2025 has only an underemployment rate.
- Quezon 2025 has no underemployment rate.
- No source URLs or publication dates per value, so point-in-time availability cannot be verified yet.
