SPECIAL RELEASE The Consumer Price Index in Lucena City, July 2022 (2018=100)

Reference Number: 

SR STAT 2022 – 023

Release Date: 

Tuesday, August 9, 2022

CPI, Year-on-Year

  • The Consumer Price Index (CPI) in Lucena City for the month of July 2022 was posted at 114.2 index points.
  • The CPI increased by 6.3 index points from last year’s figure of 107.9.

CPI by Commodity Group, Year-on-Year

  • Alcoholic beverages and tobacco showed the highest CPI (165.9 index points) among the commodity groups. The group of financial services followed with 146.0 index points.
  • Recreation, sport and culture registered the least CPI of 100.7 index points followed by information and communication with 100.9 index points.

Inflation Rate, Year-on-Year

  • The inflation rate (IR) in the city was recorded at 5.8 percent during the reference month.
  • It increased by 0.7 percentage points from the previous month’s figure of 5.1 percent and 1.3 percentage points from the rate of 4.5 percent a year ago.

Inflation Rate by Commodity Group, Year-on-Year

  • By commodity group, IR was highest for transport posted at 25.8 percent during the month.
  • Housing, water, electricity, gas and other fuels (10.9 percent) exhibited the second highest IR, followed by health (6.6 percent).
  • The rest of the commodity groups, except for information and communication; and financial services, had IR ranging from 1.0 percent (furnishing, household equipment and routine household maintenance) to 5.6 percent (alcoholic beverages and tobacco).
