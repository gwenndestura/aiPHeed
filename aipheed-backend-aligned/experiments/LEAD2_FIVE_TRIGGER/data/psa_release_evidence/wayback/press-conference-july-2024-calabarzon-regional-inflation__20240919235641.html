Press Conference on the July 2024 CALABARZON Regional Inflation

  1. Home >
  3. Press Conference on the July 2024 CALABARZON Regional Inflation
Release-Date
Wednesday, August 21, 2024
Reference-Number
2024-0400-PR37

The Philippine Statistics Authority – Regional Statistical Services Office IV-A announces the conduct of Press Conference on the July 2024 CALABARZON Regional Inflation Report, which will be held on 12 August 2024 (Monday) at 10:00 AM. Invited media, regional line agencies and provincial statistical offices in the region are expected to be virtually present during this event through video conferencing using Zoom.

PSA RSSO IV-A Regional Director Charito C. Armonia will be the resource person for the event.

The press conference will be livestreamed on the PSA Region IV-A Official Facebook page using the hashtag #CALABARZONCPI.

Details of the video conference will be sent via email.


(SGD.)
CHARITO C. ARMONIA
Regional Director

COB /JAS/ SNE
