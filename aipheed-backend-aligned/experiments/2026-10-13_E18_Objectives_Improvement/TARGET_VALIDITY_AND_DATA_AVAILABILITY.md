# Target Validity and Data Availability (E18)

| Quantity | What it is | Valid claim |
|---|---|---|
| SWS Balance-Luzon hunger | **One regional survey value per quarter** (an area wider than CALABARZON) | "Regional hunger level" |
| Provincial food CPI YoY (F) | PSA measurement of food-price inflation | "Provincial food inflation". **Not food insecurity.** |
| D = F − five-province mean | A derived comparison between provinces | "Relative food-price pressure". **Not food insecurity.** |
| Label HIGH if H + 2D > 14.6 | **A constructed composite indicator** | "Constructed stress indicator". **Not independently measured provincial food insecurity.** |

**No target was relabelled to improve results.**

## Can additional eligible data validate a province-level food-insecurity claim?

This draws on E13 and E15 records (FNRI search summaries, project documents and code). No PSA, SWS or RSSO page was opened.

| Source | Province-level? | Repeated? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| FNRI ENNS 2018/2019/2021 rolling FIES/HFIAS | Yes (provincial weights) | **Once per province** | Not in the project. Needs an FNRI microdata request. **Cross-sectional only.** |
| FNRI NNS 2023, 2025 | Regional | Biennial | 2025 regional value only (secondary source). **NNS 2019 and 2023 CALABARZON values (named in Objective 5): not available or verified.** |
| PSA subsistence incidence | Yes | Triennial (2021, 2023; 2025 released 2026-08-21, not in the project) | Imprecise (CV 17–53%). Income-based. Descriptive only. |
| PSA CBMS food-shortage items | Municipal or barangay | Per round from 2022 | **Not verified, not in the project.** The best candidate if rounds repeat. |
| DA- or LGU-run FIES module | Provincial | As designed | Does not exist yet. The only route to a temporal validation. |

## Precise missing evidence

**To validate a provincial food-insecurity claim**, the study needs:

- a provincial food-insecurity measure for all 5 provinces;
- repeated over at least 5–8 periods;
- with sampling errors;
- with verified release dates;
- not built from aiPHeed's inputs.

**To evaluate the constructed label reliably** (Objective 2-V), the study needs **more quarters with LOW cases**. 2026-Q3 onward requires your authorization of July–December 2026 data and, later, 2027 data.
