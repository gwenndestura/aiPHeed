# E87 ready-to-send data requests

These are drafts for the researchers to sign and send. Nothing has been requested or obtained yet. Bracketed fields
must be filled in.

**Routes**

| Request | Sent to | Alternative route |
|---|---|---|
| A. PSA CBMS 2024 | PSA Census Planning and Coordination Division, `cbms@psa.gov.ph`, using the **CBMS Data Request Form** (the form asks for the exact data items) | eFOI portal (foi.gov.ph), agency PSA. Copy PSA RSSO IV-A, which presented the 2024 CBMS results to CALABARZON LGUs. |
| B. DOST-FNRI | Office of the Director, DOST-FNRI | eFOI portal, agency FNRI. A precedent exists: "Raw data of ENNS 2021" was answered by FNRI. |
| C. SWS | SWS Survey Data Library | — |

SWS says all its surveys are open to research without sponsor permission, with materials supplied at a cost-based
charge.

## A. PSA — CBMS 2024 aggregates for the 142 CALABARZON cities and municipalities

> **To:** Census Planning and Coordination Division, Philippine Statistics Authority (cbms@psa.gov.ph)
> **Cc:** PSA Regional Statistical Services Office IV-A
> **Subject:** Request for 2024 CBMS city/municipal aggregates — CALABARZON (academic research)
>
> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> We are [names], [degree program], [university], conducting thesis research titled *aiPHeed: a food-insecurity
> early-warning system for the Department of Agriculture RFO IV-A*. We respectfully request, through the CBMS Data
> Request Form (attached), the following **aggregate** 2024 CBMS indicators for each of the 142 cities and
> municipalities of Region IV-A (identified by PSGC code):
>
> 1. Share of households with moderate-or-severe and with severe food insecurity, as PSA classifies them from the
>    CBMS food-security items. If no classification is published, give the share answering "yes" to each
>    food-security item (worried about food; ate less; ran out of food; went hungry without eating; went a whole day
>    without eating). State the reference period.
> 2. The number of households interviewed per LGU, and standard errors or coefficients of variation where available.
> 3. Share of households below the poverty and food thresholds (CBMS definitions), and the main source of household
>    income (share with wage, agriculture or fishing, and remittance as main source).
> 4. The date of enumeration (month range) for each LGU.
>
> **No household-level, barangay-level or personally identifiable data are requested.** The figures will be used only
> to check, under a pre-registered analysis plan, how well the system's municipal vulnerability ranking agrees with
> observed municipal food insecurity. Results will be reported as research findings with PSA as the source. The data
> will not be redistributed, will be stored on an encrypted university computer, and will be deleted at the end of
> the study if PSA requires. We will sign any data-use agreement PSA requires.
>
> Preferred format: CSV or Excel. Contact: [name, email, phone]. Adviser: [name].
>
> Respectfully,
> [signatures]

## Data dependencies and what each request unlocks

| Request | Unlocks | Exact use |
|---|---|---|
| A (CBMS 2024) | The first municipal food-insecurity observation (cross-section) | Validates the 142-LGU ranking; calibrates local estimates |
| B (FNRI provincial FIES) | Direct provincial food-insecurity levels for all five provinces | Provincial face validity; anchors any provincial target |
| C (SWS CALABARZON microdata) | A region-specific quarterly hunger series, plus noisy provincial readings | Replaces Balance Luzon in the outcome; more relevant timing and level for Lead-2 training |
