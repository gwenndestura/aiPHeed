What these outputs are

(including the LightGBM food-price-pressure forecast), household vulnerability (poverty and density), climate and food production,

compared with its own earlier values, and domains are weighted by how closely they tracked observed regional hunger. No direct
provincial food-insecurity outcome exists, so it is a proxy estimate for planning, not a probability and not a measured
food-insecurity rate.

Indicative risk, not official classification

Outputs are relative ordinal estimates of potential risk. They are not definitive determinations of food insecurity status and must not be
read as official government assessments or policy recommendations.

Proxy label, not a direct measurement

No publicly accessible province-quarter food insecurity label exists in the Philippines. The training target is a composite of SWS hunger
prevalence, which has no province-level resolution, and PSA food CPI deviation, which reflects supply-side price conditions rather than
household hunger experience directly.

Reviewed before publication

Province-quarter figures pass through analyst review. A withheld figure is excluded from this report and from the regional average.

Scope of this assessment

This assessment reports findings only. It does not recommend interventions: no validated mapping exists between this indicator
and a response protocol. Pair it with the Department's own standing guidance.
