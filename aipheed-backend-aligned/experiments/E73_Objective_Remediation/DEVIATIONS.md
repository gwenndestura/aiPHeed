# E73 deviations and clarifications (recorded before any comparison is computed)

1. **Survey quarter of an OCTA round (clarifies PROTOCOL §2b).** OCTA's own labels do not follow fieldwork: the July 2026 round is called "Q2 2026". So the round's quarter is the quarter that contains the majority of its fieldwork days, and rounds in the same quarter are averaged, as for SWS. Example: 30 Sep – 4 Oct 2023 → 2023-Q4.
2. **Values reported only as "from X percent" in a later article.** These are admitted with the later article as their source, because the previous round is named in that article.
