## Conclusion

**What CBMS 2024 LGU food-security data are**
- They exist: one cross-section, 2024.
- They are released to LGUs through turnover ceremonies, from September 2025 in CALABARZON.
- They are not published as a public LGU table.

**How to obtain them**
- A PSA data request (RSSO 4A), or
- a request to each LGU, since LGUs own their CBMS data after turnover.

**What they can do**
- At most one cross-sectional check of the provincial and LGU rankings for 2024.
- They cannot validate quarterly forecasts.
