# E121 design issues (found during implementation; none applied; owner approval needed)

The frozen protocol is implemented exactly as written. The issues below are **not fixed in FIRI3**, so the protocol hash and the E120 reproduction stay intact. Each needs an owner decision and, if accepted, a new protocol version.

| # | Issue | Evidence | Effect | Proposed fix (for approval) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| DI-1 | **Province differences come almost entirely from the two rank-based indicators** (EHS employment ranks, PSV poverty). The dynamic indicators are pooled percentiles, so their province means sit near 0.5. | Province mean indicator scores on the 11 comparable origins: AFP 0.44–0.57, CEC 0.49–0.51, FMA 0.41–0.54, but EHS 0.28–0.76 and PSV 0.1–0.9. Without PSV, the province ranking changes (ρ 0.77); without EHS, ρ 0.90. | A province's FIRI3 rank mostly reflects its annual labour-market and poverty position, not current conditions. Batangas ranks first in 7 of 11 comparable quarters, because its underemployment is 31–40%. | Report two parts beside the overall score: a **conditions score** (FMA, AFP, CEC, FIS: over-time change) and a **structural score** (EHS, PSV: cross-province position). Or keep one FIRI3 but label rank-based indicators in the explanation. |
| DI-2 | **Scores with different indicator sets are not fully comparable** | PSV joins at 2025-Q3. Its entry shifts individual provinces by −0.04 to +0.10 (composition effect; Quezon +0.10, as PSV = 0.9). FIS joins only when there are ≥ 5 articles. Mean FIRI3: 0.513 with 4 indicators vs 0.418–0.419 with 5–6, but these are different periods. | Comparing a province before and after 2025-Q3, or two provinces with different sets, mixes data availability with conditions | Always report the **common-core score** (FMA, AFP, CEC, EHS) beside FIRI3, and flag set changes. Or require the same set for cross-province comparisons. The geographic report already uses only same-set origins. |
| DI-3 | **EHS becomes unavailable for 2026-Q2 and 2026-Q3** | The rule takes the *latest* published year and requires all five provinces. 2025 lacks Batangas unemployment and Quezon underemployment, although 2024 is complete. | 10 province-quarters lose EHS. The FIRI3 for those quarters rests on FMA, AFP, CEC (+ PSV/FIS). | Fall back to the latest **complete** year (2024), with its year shown |
| DI-4 | **Low verification share** | Contributing features with archived release dates: provincial mean 8.6%, regional 16.9%. Most dates are PSA release rules. | A strict (verified-only) FIRI3 is not computable at present | Obtain PSA release calendars (owner request) |
| DI-5 | **Leave-one-indicator-out range is wide relative to the bands** | Mean range 0.13 (provincial) and 0.14 (regional). The "elevated" band starts 0.12–0.14 above 0.5. | A single indicator can move a score across a band | Already reported with every score. Consider showing the range on any display. |
| DI-6 | **Batangas underemployment 39.5% (2024) and 31.0% (2023)** vs 8–14% elsewhere | Owner file (E116); 2024 marked preliminary | Drives Batangas's high EHS rank | Verify against the PSA provincial LFS table before adoption |
| DI-7 | **Regional FIRI3 is short** | Computable for 10 quarters (2024-Q2+). A single regional series needs 8 earlier 2021+ observations of its year-on-year change. | Little history for any regional comparison | None within the 2021+ rule; it grows by one quarter per release |
