1.5.2 Specific Objectives 1. To design a two-tier pipeline combining zero-shot XLM-RoBER Ta Filipino-English sentiment extraction and BER Topic unsupervised topic modeling to produce the bias-corrected Food Stress Sentiment Index and latent food insecurity risk patterns per province-quarter from a Filipino-English news corpus. 2. To evaluate the performance of the proposed LightGBM forecasting model using the following metrics: I. Accuracy , to measure overall correctness of high- and low-risk province-level classifications II. Precision and Recall, to assess the model's ability to correctly identify high-risk provinces while minimizing false alarms and missed detections respectively 12 III. F1-Score, to capture the balance between Precision and Recall given the binary classification task IV. AUC-ROC, to evaluate the model's ability to distinguish between high- and low-risk provinces across classification thresholds V. Forecast performance across multiple lead times via walk-forward expanding-window cross-validation to empirically determine the furthest horizon at which reliable forecasting is achievable 3. To produce spatially disaggregated municipal and city food insecurity risk rankings from validated province-level forecasts, using PSA published poverty incidence and population density as vulnerability weights. 4. To compare the effectiveness of the proposed LightGBM forecasting model against the following baseline approaches: I. Naive persistence baseline II. PSA-only indicator baseline III. NLP-only sentiment baseline IV. Logistic regression baseline 5. 5. To deploy a public geospatial dashboard presenting forecast maps and early-warning alerts, evaluate it under the ISO/IEC 25010 software quality model with DA Region IV-A evaluators, and validate its forecasts against DOST -FNRI NNS 2019 and 2023 CALABARZON prevalence figures as a face-validity anchor . 13
