### A2

**Query:** "Philippines food security emergency declaration 2025 Department of Agriculture criteria rice price RA 12078".

**Result.** DA Department Circular No. 3 (3 Feb 2025) declared a food security emergency on rice. The trigger is price-based: the National Price Coordinating Council's "extraordinary increase" test (rice inflation above the food-inflation target band and in double digits).

**URLs:**
- https://www.da.gov.ph/da-chief-declares-food-security-emergency-on-rice/
- https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1243265
- https://www.philstar.com/business/2025/02/03/2418959/da-declares-food-security-emergency-curb-rising-rice-prices

### A3

**Query:** "IPC acute food insecurity analysis Philippines BARMM 2023 area phase classification 20 percent households threshold".

**Result.**
- IPC area rule: at least 20 % of the population in a phase or worse.
- NNC coordinates IPC in the Philippines.
- The Philippine IPC country page counts provinces by phase, from the ARMM era.
- No CALABARZON analysis and no forecast tolerance.
