## 7. Does MODEL-B2 meaningfully outperform MODEL-A and the baselines?

**No.**

**Primary analysis (A-origin):**

- MODEL-B2 is the **weakest non-trivial predictor**. Its accuracy (0.571) is below MODEL-A, the Equation 3 rule, carry-forward and always-HIGH.
- Its deficits against carry-forward and always-HIGH have bootstrap intervals that exclude zero.
- Its balanced accuracy (0.615) and AUC (0.688) exceed MODEL-A's (0.422, 0.505).
- On balanced accuracy it is below the Equation 3 rule (0.755) and carry-forward (0.771). All of these rest on 3 LOW rows.

**Leakage-affected analysis (A-global):**

- MODEL-B2 ties carry-forward (24/35).
- It is 2 rows above MODEL-A and the Equation 3 rule, a difference that is not distinguishable (bootstrap CIs include zero).
- It is below always-HIGH.

## 8. Class imbalance, collapse and missing data

**Class imbalance:**

- Under A-origin, the fold medians lag behind the rising stress levels of 2023–2025, so 32 of 35 test rows exceed τ_T.
- Training sets are balanced (HIGH share 0.49–0.50) but the test set is not. The rising origin-relative threshold is not enough to follow the upward trend.
- Persistence-type rules (carry-forward, always-HIGH) exploit this. That is a property of the period, not evidence of forecasting skill.

**Prediction collapse:**

| Model | Quarter | Test HIGH | Predicted HIGH | Correct |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| MODEL-B2 (A-origin) | 2023-Q4 | 4/5 | 0 | 1/5 |
| MODEL-B2 (A-origin) | 2025-Q1 | 5/5 | 1 | 1/5 |
| MODEL-A | 2025-Q1 | 5/5 | 0 | 0/5 |

- MODEL-A predicts HIGH in 30 of 35 rows. Its accuracy mostly reflects the base rate (balanced accuracy 0.422, below chance).

**Missing data:**

- The Equation 3 rule needed no majority fallback (0 rows).
- No fold was degenerate.
- LightGBM handled NaN natively; nothing was imputed.

**Undefined metrics:**

- ROC-AUC, Brier and within-quarter AUC are undefined for the rules and references.
- S5 has one quarter, so no bootstrap interval is defined.
- HIGH recall is defined in every block, because every block contains HIGH rows.

## 10. Limitations

- **Reused quarters:** this is an exploratory development estimate. Every quarter was used before, and the 70.9% training-only result was seen before this design was chosen (protocol §1).
- **Effective sample about 7.** The A-origin balanced accuracy and AUC rest on 3 LOW rows; A-global rests on 5.
- **Not real-time:** inputs use the WD1 provincial-CPI date proxy and revised climate data (WD2).
- **Unconfirmed SWS values:** June, September and November 2025 are each confirmed by one reading only. November 2025 is the 2025-Q4 label.
- **Adviser decisions pending:** A-origin as primary, the minimum-window reading, D3 respondent composition, and WD1/WD2/WD5.
- **No result here supports a > 75% accuracy claim.** The only model with point accuracy above 0.75 under A-origin is MODEL-A (0.771), and that is base-rate driven. The trivial always-HIGH rule scores higher than every model.
