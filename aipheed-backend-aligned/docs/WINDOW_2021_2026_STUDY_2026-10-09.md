The test covers 8 quarters (2023-Q3 to 2025-Q4) and 40 province-quarters.

Supporting points:

- P4 has an AUC of 0.875 and ranks provinces well within a quarter (within-quarter AUC 0.83).
- The MODEL-B2 configuration is also the **procedure's own final choice** when run on all 2021–2025 data.
- **What the data can support:** a confident claim of accuracy above 75% needs at least **35/40 (87.5%)** correct. If the true accuracy were 80%, the chance of observing that would be 0.16.

**What does improve within the period:**

| Improvement | Effect |
|---|---|
| Data corrections | Removed artifacts from the inputs; 100% of the 2021+ inputs are now observed or honestly missing |
| Eq. 3 province term (`cpi_rel`) | Within-quarter AUC rises from about 0.63 (original MODEL-B2) to about 0.79–0.83 |

**Conclusions:**

- **Predictions are still mostly regional and temporal:** 69–88% of the probability variance lies between quarters.
- **They now carry real province information.** Within-quarter AUC is about 0.78–0.83, against 0.63 for the original MODEL-B2.
- **The quarter-level regime component dominates accuracy.** It is fairly predictable at the regime level (7 of 8 quarters), but how far each quarter sits from the threshold is not predictable.
- **The province component is the weaker source of signal.** Known SWS plus the true province term gives only 65%.

## 6. Best defensible model configuration

**The MODEL-B2 configuration on the corrected 2021+ frame:**

- `gov_c` (44 inputs), LightGBM defaults, `scale_pos_weight = (1−p)/p` from the training rows;
- strict timing; window rule W; threshold 14.345.

Why this one:

1. It is the pre-registered procedure's choice when run on all 2021–2025 data, the unique top choice apart from one tie (inner score 0.818).
2. It is the governing MODEL-B2 configuration, minus the artifacts.
3. It is the best of the named models on HIGH recall (0.81) and Brier score (0.157), with within-quarter AUC 0.79.

**Its 80.0% (32/40) is not the unbiased estimate.** The unbiased estimate is P4's 72.5%, which includes the cost of choosing a configuration. The configuration is not deployed; adopting it in production needs your approval.

## 8. Handling of 2026

- **No 2026 labels exist.** The latest SWS round in the project is 2025-Q4, released 2026-05-12. Nothing from 2026 was used as a label.
- **Frozen, label-blind forecasts** for 2026-Q1 and 2026-Q2 are stored in `FROZEN_2026_FORECASTS.csv` (SHA-256 recorded) for three candidates:
  - the procedure's choice (= the MODEL-B2 configuration);
  - the Eq. 3 rule;
  - the MODEL-B2 configuration.
- **Inputs used:** observed 2025-Q4 government values (strict timing), 2026-Q1 news, and SWS 2025-Q1 (24.0), the latest round released before each origin.
- **Caveat:** the forecasts were issued after their origin dates, from current data vintages. They are label-blind, but not as-released.
- **What they predict:** the Eq. 3 rule calls every province HIGH. The MODEL-B2 configuration calls 4 of 5 HIGH in 2026-Q1, and 3 of 5 in 2026-Q2 (Quezon and Rizal LOW).
- **Not issued:** 2026-Q3 and 2026-Q4. They need provincial food CPI for 2026-Q1 and Q2, which the project does not hold (`psa_indicators` ends 2025-Q4).
- **Scoring rule:** when SWS publishes the 2026 rounds, score with Eq. 3 at the frozen threshold, against persistence and clock-only, under `experiments/2026-10-09_generalization/PROSPECTIVE_EVALUATION_PLAN.md`. Report every quarter.
